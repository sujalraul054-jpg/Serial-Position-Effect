Serial Position Effect

Serial Position Effect

People tend to remember the first and last items of a list better than the items in the middle. This experiment records how well you recall two word lists and compares the two.

Procedure

  1. The session has two levels.
  2. In each level, ten words appear one at a time. You cannot pause or skip.
  3. After each level, type every word you remember.
  4. When both levels are finished, your results are shown and recorded.

Type every word you remember, separated by spaces, commas or new lines. The order does not matter.

Recall by serial position, all participants

Settings

Appearance

Applies from the next level that starts.

Data

Deleting removes sessions from the spreadsheet and the graph, and cannot be undone.
